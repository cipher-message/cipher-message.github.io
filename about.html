About Cipher Message

Cipher Message is a quiet messenger built for school life. It keeps chats, groups and calls in one calm place, without the noise.

What you can do

  • Message friends one-to-one or in group chats.
  • Make voice and video calls, including group calls with screen sharing.
  • Share files, voice messages and reactions.
  • Make it yours with chat backgrounds, avatars, light and dark themes.
  • Use Cipher AI for quick help when you want it.

Why it was made

School chat apps are often loud, cluttered or hard to reach. Cipher aims to be simple, calm and easy to open wherever you are, with privacy treated as a basic feature rather than an extra.
